Ctrl+T opens the `/ship` plan: every ticket with the landed ones dimmed and the one in flight marked, beside the agent's own todo list, since the two are different granularities of the same work. The fullscreen reader can copy what it is showing with `c` — which covers a diff, the one thing `/copy` cannot address, because tool cards are deliberately outside its index. Its footer leads with how to leave and how to copy, so a narrow terminal cuts a navigation hint rather than the way out.
