A workflow shows its progress while it runs. `/ship`'s ralph loop spends minutes per round and printed nothing until the whole run returned; the surface now opens the run with its name, prints a line as each round settles, names the round still running in the working line, and closes with the stop reason. A settled round is a door: clicking it enters that round's child session, where its todos and tools are.
