`/ship` continues after HITL, dispatches a Landing wave, and always shows the two-ring panorama (`/goal` stays disarmed). The Web panorama is one loopback URL for the TTY session: the page polls the live graph and is not rebound when `/ship` continues or a spec appears. Decision tickets join the inner ring as soon as they are written (a published map pointer is not a ticket and does not hide local wayfinder files); landing tickets join the outer ring from the approved plan. Phase contracts are the injected prompt; `/ship` does not look up wayfinder, grill-me, to-spec, to-tickets, or tdd skills.
