The box has undo. Ctrl+Z or Ctrl+_ takes the last edit back — a typed word at a time, a paste or a kill whole, a run of Backspace as one — and on a terminal speaking the kitty keyboard protocol Ctrl+Shift+Z brings it back again. A submission starts the history over; Esc Esc still recalls the sent line. `?` lists the key.
