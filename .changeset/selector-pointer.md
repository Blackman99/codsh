Selectors answer the mouse. A click takes a row in `/model`, `/resume`, `/jump`, `/copy`, `/view`, and every `ask_user_question` — a multi-select row toggles the way Space does, and the "type your own" row opens free text. Resting on a row underlines it without moving the `❯`, so the pointer never changes what Enter would take, and a press commits only where it is released. Approvals stay keyboard-only, and the transcript is untouched: press-to-anchor, drag-to-extend, release-to-copy work exactly as before.
