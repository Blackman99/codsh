The wheel scrolls the list it is over. An open selector or completion menu now holds a window of its own while the wheel moves it, so looking further down a list never changes what Enter would take — and any key brings the window back to the marked row. A scroll from the keyboard is left to the transcript wherever the pointer happens to rest, because a scroll now carries where it turned and one without a place did not come from the mouse.
